      How it works

      Patterns, not mind reading.

      ScamShield checks the message against known warning signs and explains each match. It never decides for you and never sends your text anywhere.

      • PressureDeadlines, threats, and “act now” language.
      • SecretsRequests for passwords, PINs, or one-time codes.
      • PaymentGift cards, crypto, transfers, and release fees.
      • LinksShorteners, lookalike domains, raw IPs, and risky endings.
      • AccessRemote-access tools, APKs, and macro-enabled files.
      • BaitPrizes, guaranteed returns, and too-good offers.

      What this cannot do

      This is guidance, not a guarantee. A message can be a scam without matching these patterns, and a legitimate message can use urgent language. If money or personal data is involved, verify the sender through an official app or a phone number you look up yourself.

      ScamShield is free and open source. Built to be useful, not to collect data.
